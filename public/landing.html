    Choose the exact date and time it may open. Add trusted people if you want them to agree too.

// What is guaranteed

  • Nobody can open it before the chosen time
  • Only the trusted people you chose can help open it, once each
  • Every attempt, even blocked ones, is recorded in public and cannot be deleted
  • If the file is changed, it will not open and you are told
  • Your exact location is never taken without your OK

// Honest limits

  • If our service and enough of your trusted people all work together, they could open it early
  • For "just the time" files, our service keeps the key until then
  • The person who locks the file already has the original
  • Once it opens, someone can still take a photo of it
  • Rough location from the internet connection can be wrong
