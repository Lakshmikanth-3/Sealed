Capabilities

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

  1. 01

    Lock until a time

    Pick a file and the exact second it may open. Before that, the smart contract on NEAR refuses every request.

  2. 02

    Split between trusted people

    Split the key into parts. Choose how many people must agree, like 3 of 5. Fewer than that learn nothing.

  3. 03

    Private live chat

    Chat with one person like WhatsApp, end-to-end encrypted. Messages can be sealed until a time or seen only once.

  4. 04

    Exam papers with leak tracing

    One paper per centre. If a copy leaks, paste it in and see which centre it came from.

  5. 05

    Every attempt on the record

    Early, blocked or allowed, every attempt is written to NEAR. The owner gets an alert with time and device.

What is guaranteed

  • Nobody can open it before the chosen time
  • Only the trusted people you chose can help open it, once each
  • Every attempt, even blocked ones, is recorded in public and cannot be deleted
  • If the file is changed, it will not open and you are told
  • Your exact location is never taken without your OK

Honest limits

  • If our service and enough of your trusted people all work together, they could open it early
  • For "just the time" files, our service keeps the key until then
  • The person who locks the file already has the original
  • Once it opens, someone can still take a photo of it
  • Rough location from the internet connection can be wrong
