// How it works

  1. 01

    Record

    Log trips, pay, ratings, penalties and deactivation notices with the original screenshot. Everything stays on the device.

  2. 02

    Lock on NEAR

    Each day becomes a salted Merkle root. The worker's own wallet signs it into the contract. No personal data goes on-chain.

  3. 03

    Check the company

    Load the company's statement. Appeal flags underpaid trips, missing work and unexplained charges, and records the result.

  4. 04

    Share & verify

    Disclose only chosen records through an expiring link. Reviewers check signatures, receipts and proofs against public RPC.

// What a reviewer checks

  • Trusted contract and network
  • Worker's NEAR account signed the anchor
  • Finalized, successful receipt
  • Merkle proof for every disclosed record
  • Original screenshot fingerprints
  • Totals recomputed, not trusted

// What it does not claim

  • That a record is true, only that it existed unchanged since it was anchored
  • A worker's legal identity
  • Automatic capture from company apps
  • Legal advice or a ruling
